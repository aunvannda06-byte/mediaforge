FAQ

What video formats are supported?

Playback depends on your browser. You can export WebM today. MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and GIF output need an FFmpeg engine, which is not connected yet.

Can I extract MP3 from a video?

Yes. Choose MP3 in the Video Converter, or Extract WAV in the Video Editor.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes. Add several files and press Convert All.

Can I edit videos directly in the browser?

Yes. Split, trim, filters, text, an image overlay and WebM export all run in your browser.

Can I edit multiple audio tracks?

Not yet. The Audio Editor works on one file at a time, with undo.

Can I change bitrate and sample rate?

Yes for MP3 bitrate (64 to 320 kbps) and for sample rate. MP3 is capped at 48 kHz.

Can I convert stereo audio to mono?

Yes, in the Audio Converter.

Does MediaForge support 4K?

With Original resolution it keeps your video's size if your browser can play it. Speed depends on your device.

Are my files stored permanently?

No. Files stay in your browser and disappear when you close the tab.

How long does processing take?

Audio is quick. WebM conversion and export run in real time, so a 2 minute video takes about 2 minutes.

Documentation

Video Editor

Open a video, split, duplicate or delete clips, apply filters, add text, then export WebM (or GIF with the server).

Audio Editor

Drag on the waveform to select, then trim, cut, add EQ, fades and effects, and export WAV or MP3 (M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, AIFF and WMA with the FFmpeg server). Any audio or video file can be opened; formats your browser cannot read are converted by the server automatically.

Converters

Add files, choose a format, then Convert All. Use Cancel, Retry and Download All for batches.

FFmpeg server

Run npm install && node server.js to unlock MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, GIF, AAC, M4A, OGG and FLAC.
